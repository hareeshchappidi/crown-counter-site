Privacy Policy

Crown Counter · Effective 17 October 2026

In short: Crown Counter collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your Apple Watch.

What the app stores

Crown Counter saves the counters you create on your Apple Watch: their names, colours, counts, goals and settings, plus a daily history of how much you counted. This lets the app and its watch face complications show your counts.

What we collect

Nothing. Crown Counter does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections.

Third parties

Crown Counter contains no third-party code, SDKs or services that could access your data.

Backups

Apple may include app data in your Apple Watch and iPhone backups, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own settings. We cannot access these backups.

Deleting your data

Delete a counter in the app to remove it and its history. Deleting the app from your Apple Watch removes all of its data.

Children

Crown Counter is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
